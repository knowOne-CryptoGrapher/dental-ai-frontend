## 2. Service Description

2.1 The Clinic's subscription is provisioned under Simplex Dental's `founding_clinic_basic` plan. As configured today, this plan includes:

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Calls per month | 250 |
| Providers | up to 5 |
| Locations | 1 |
| Users | up to 5 |

## 3. Founding Clinic Pricing Terms

3.1 The Clinic's subscription fee is $299 CAD per month, billed monthly.

3.2 This rate is locked for the life of the Clinic's continuous, uninterrupted subscription and will not increase. If the Clinic's subscription is cancelled or lapses and the Clinic later re-subscribes, that re-subscription will be under whatever pricing and plan availability exists at that time — the Founding Clinic rate is not automatically reinstated.

3.3 The Founding Clinic Program is limited to ten (10) participating clinics and, for this initial cohort, is available to clinics located in British Columbia only.

## 7. Data Retention & Post-Termination Handling

**7.1 Active-Service Retention.** While the Clinic is an active customer, Simplex Dental retains Clinic and patient data in accordance with the retention period configured by the Clinic from its practice dashboard, defaulting to seven (7) years, consistent with common Canadian dental record retention expectations. This setting governs Simplex Dental's own handling of data during the active service relationship and may be adjusted by the Clinic within the product at any time.

**7.2 Post-Termination Retention.** Upon termination of this Agreement, whether by the Clinic or by Simplex Dental, the Clinic's data will be retained for ninety (90) days following the effective date of termination. This 90-day period is a grace window during which the Clinic may request an export of its data. After the 90-day window has elapsed, the Clinic's data will be deleted or anonymized from active systems and backups in accordance with Simplex Dental's retention procedures, unless a different retention period is required by applicable law or otherwise agreed in writing between the parties.

**7.3 Data Export.** During the 90-day post-termination window, the Clinic may request an export of its data by contacting support. On request, Simplex Dental will provide the Clinic's patient, provider, appointment, and call log records in a standard exportable format. The Clinic is encouraged to request this export promptly following termination and before the 90-day window closes.

**7.4 Clinic's Independent Regulatory Recordkeeping Obligations.** The retention periods described in this Section reflect Simplex Dental's own data handling practices while providing the service and following termination — they do not replace, satisfy, or substitute for the Clinic's own independent obligations, as a health care provider, to retain patient records for the period required by applicable professional regulatory requirements. Such regulatory retention periods may be materially longer than the retention periods described in this Section. The Clinic remains solely responsible for maintaining any records required for its own regulatory compliance, both during the term of this Agreement and following its termination, and should request an export under Section 7.3 above to the extent needed to meet those obligations.

8.2 Upon termination, the Clinic's access to the Service will be disabled at the end of the then-current billing period, unless the Agreement is terminated for cause, as part of Simplex Dental's standard offboarding process.

8.3 Data handling upon termination is governed by Section 7 above.

9.2 For ongoing support during the term of this Agreement, the Clinic may contact Simplex Dental at support@frontdeskdentalai.com. Support is provided on a best-effort basis. This Agreement does not include formal response-time commitments or a dedicated ticketing system; any such commitments would be described in a separate Service Level Agreement, if and when one is adopted.
